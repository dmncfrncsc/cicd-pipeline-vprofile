## Historical planning cost comparison

The following examples were checked against pricing pages on 2026-10-09 and were planning illustrations, not a quote, approval, or verified capacity result. Assumptions: `us-east-1`, Linux On-Demand T3 instances, 730 compute hours in an average month, and an illustrative 48-hour run. They are before credits and taxes and exclude T3 surplus CPU-credit charges, backups/snapshots, NAT data processing and internet transfer unless stated, S3 requests, and additional security services.

| Example | Compute per hour | 48 hours | 730 hours | Memory |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| One `t3.large` | $0.0835 | $4.01 | $60.96 | 8 GiB |
| Four `t3.small` | $0.0836 total | $4.01 total | $61.03 total | 8 GiB total (2 GiB each) |

At the cited gp3 example rate of $0.08/GiB-month, a placeholder 20-GiB root volume was estimated at $1.60/month for one host or $6.40/month for four; the 48-hour prorated estimates were about $0.11 and $0.43. A single 83,278,692-byte WAR was estimated at about 0.0776 GiB and $0.0018 per retained copy per month at the cited S3 Standard rate, before requests.

The network comparison used reported example rates of $0.045 per NAT Gateway-hour, $0.045 per GB processed, and $0.005 per public IPv4 address-hour. One NAT Gateway plus one public IPv4 was estimated at $2.40 fixed for 48 hours and $36.50 for 730 hours, before processing/transfer. The public-subnet fallback's IPv4 estimates were $0.24/$3.65 for one address and $0.96/$14.60 for four, at 48/730 hours. Three interface endpoints in one Availability Zone were estimated at $1.44/$21.90 before processing. Required endpoint count and package-site connectivity were unverified. A current N. Virginia NAT quote was not confirmed; all rates must be recalculated before a cost gate.
